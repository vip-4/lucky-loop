Privacy & fair play

Small game. Small footprint.

Lucky Loop is designed to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics, ads, or third-party requests — and the random results are generated on your device.

What is stored on your device

The game saves three values in your browser’s localStorage under the key luckyLoop.v1:

  • Your current credit balance.
  • Your selected bet size.
  • Your sound preference.

This information never leaves your browser and is not sent to a server. It exists so your balance and settings survive a refresh. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes it permanently.

What is not collected

The game does not ask for your name, email address, location, or payment details, and it does not create a profile of your play. There is no account system and no cloud sync. The page loads no third-party fonts, scripts, pixels, or embeds.

Like most static websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical data such as IP addresses and user-agent strings in server logs to deliver and protect the site. Lucky Loop does not combine that data with game activity.

Cookies and analytics

No cookies are set by Lucky Loop. No analytics platform, advertising network, or session recorder is installed. The only browser storage is the local game state described above.

How the game stays fair

Each reel has a fixed 24-stop strip and is drawn independently using the browser’s Web Crypto API. The code uses rejection sampling to avoid modulo bias, so every stop has an equal chance. Results are never altered based on your balance, previous wins, or bet size.

The published paytable produces a theoretical return of 96.30% over an unlimited number of spins. Individual sessions are volatile, and no outcome is due. The game does not simulate near misses.

Play money, by design

Credits are virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged. Lucky Loop does not accept deposits, offer withdrawals, or award prizes. It is entertainment software for adults aged 18 and over, not a gambling service.

Changes to this policy

About the game

What is Lucky Loop?

Lucky Loop is an independent, play-money slot game built as a lightweight static web page. It uses three independent 24-stop reels and a single center payline. The rules are simple enough to learn in one spin, but the exact odds are published below so you always know what you are playing.

The whole game runs locally. There is no account, no server-side wallet, and no third-party script. Reel outcomes come from the Web Crypto API, the page makes no network requests while you play, and the playable page is under 100 KB before compression. Credits, bet size, and sound preference are the only things saved, and they stay in your browser.

How it stays fair

  • Independent reels. Every stop is drawn separately from a fixed 24-symbol strip.
  • Unbiased randomness. Rejection sampling removes modulo bias from crypto.getRandomValues.
  • No near-miss tricks. The game never adjusts results based on wins, losses, or balance.
  • Published math. The full paytable and exact probabilities are shown below.

How to play

Three steps to your first spin

No tutorial mode needed. The machine starts with 1,000 credits and remembers your bet between visits.

  1. 01

    Set your bet

    Use the − and + buttons to choose 1, 5, 10, or 25 credits per spin. Your bet is deducted when the reels start.

  2. 02

    Spin the reels

    Press Spin and watch all three reels stop on the center payline. Every spin is independent and generated locally.

  3. 03

    Match to win

    Three matching symbols pay the top prizes. Any two cherries pay 1× your bet. Winnings are added to your credits instantly.

Keyboard tip: Tab to a control and use Space or Enter. If you run out of credits, choose Reset credits to start again with 1,000.

How the 96.30% RTP is calculated

Each 24-stop reel contains 6 cherries, 5 stars, 4 clovers, 4 bells, 3 diamonds, and 2 sevens. Three-of-a-kind prizes contribute 82.23% of the return; the any-two-cherries prize adds 14.06%. Together they produce a theoretical return of 96.30% over an unlimited number of spins. Short sessions are volatile and can land anywhere around that long-run average.

What does the 96.30% RTP mean?

RTP stands for theoretical return to player. Across a very large number of spins, the paytable returns an average of 96.30 credits for every 100 credits wagered. Short sessions can be well above or below that figure because each spin is independent.

Are the spins actually random?

Yes. Each reel is drawn independently from a 24-stop strip using the browser’s Web Crypto API with unbiased rejection sampling. The code does not track losses, steer outcomes, or simulate near misses. Everything runs locally on your device.

Does Lucky Loop work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The page is responsive and works in current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on desktop and mobile. There is no app to install; a modern browser and JavaScript are all you need.

Is my progress saved?

Your credits, bet size, and sound preference are stored in your browser’s local storage so they can survive a page refresh. There is no account and no cloud sync. Clearing site data resets the game.
